What Already Happens at Coachella

  • Airbnb economy: 9,000 guests stayed in Airbnbs during Coachella in 2024 -- double the previous year. Hosts near the venue make their entire annual mortgage in one weekend.

  • Merch economy: Justin Bieber's brand did $5M in merch sales in one Coachella weekend. One vendor reported $100K per hour in merch lines.

  • On-site spend: Average per-person spend: $375. A single pizza slice costs $11. Premium pricing is the business model.

  • Creator economy: Brands pay creators thousands for a single sponsored post from the festival. For many creators, Coachella is the biggest payday of the year.

  • Local economic impact: $403M total spending across the Coachella Valley. $106M into the city of Indio alone.

How to Execute: Step by Step

Airbnb Party -- 7-Day Plan

When What to Do
Day 7 Concert announced. Book an Airbnb within 2km of venue. Budget Rs 8,000-12,000.
Day 5 Post on Instagram: 'Pre-party for [Artist] concert. Rs 2,000 cover. Limited to 50. DM to book.'
Day 3 Collect UPI payments. Confirm guest list. Buy supplies -- ice, cups, speakers, fairy lights.
Day 0 Set up 3 hours before the concert. Party runs 2-3 hours pre or post show. Clean up. Return keys.
Day +1 Post photos. Tag guests. Build the brand for the next concert.

Survival Kit -- 48-Hour Sprint

When What to Do
48 hrs before Post on Instagram: 'Concert survival kit for [Artist]. Rs 399. DM to order. Limited stock.'
36 hrs before Buy bulk: ear plugs (Rs 15 each), Pee Safe (Rs 30), electrolyte sachets (Rs 10), safety pins, meds.
24 hrs before Pack kits in zip lock bags. Add a card with your Instagram handle. Cost per kit: Rs 100-150.
Concert day Porter kits to venue area. Hand-deliver. Collect remaining payments.
Day +1 Post: '200 kits sold out.' Social proof for the next event.

Personal Paparazzi -- Concert Day Plan

When What to Do
1 week before Post: 'Paparazzi arrival photos at [Artist] concert. Rs 2,000 for 15 min shoot. DM to book.'
Day before Scout venue entrance. Find the best backdrop -- signage, crowd, lights.
Concert day Set up 2 hours before gates. Shoot arrivals. 15 min per client. Aim for 15-20 bookings.
Same night Quick edit on Lightroom mobile. Send 10-15 photos per client via WhatsApp. Fast delivery = instant posts.
Day +1 Repost client photos with permission. Their followers see your work. Next concert books itself.
