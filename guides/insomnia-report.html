THE INSOMNIA COOKIES STORY A BRAND BUILT AROUND JUST A COOKIE

One student, one dorm room, $150. Sources: Wikipedia, CNBC, Fortune, HuffPost, How I Built This (NPR), Daily Pennsylvanian.

Somewhere, someone once looked at a bakery counter and asked a simple question: why is there no real brand built entirely around the cookie? Turns out, someone already answered it. Twenty years ago, a college student asked exactly that -- and built a company now worth hundreds of millions of dollars, one warm cookie at a time.

University of Pennsylvania. THE IDEA 2003.

Fact Detail
Founder Seth Berkowitz, an economics major at Penn's Wharton School.
The spark Living in a college house with eight roommates, tired of the same late-night pizza orders, craving something sweet instead -- with zero late-night dessert delivery options anywhere nearby.
Starting capital About $150 spent on baking supplies.
The first move Baked the first batch himself and handed out free samples on Penn's Locust Walk. Instant hit -- friends said they'd pay if he delivered.
The product Just cookies. Delivered warm, late at night, 9pm to 2am -- built entirely around when people actually wanted them.

A cookie. Something sitting on every bakery counter in the world. Nobody had built a brand purely around it.

One newspaper story THE MOMENT IT TOOK OFF changed everything

Milestone Detail
The breakthrough The campus newspaper, The Daily Pennsylvanian, ran a front-page feature on the dorm-room cookie delivery business.
The jump Orders went from 3 a night to 80 a night, almost overnight.
What happened next He hired help, found a bigger kitchen, and within a couple of years secured FDA approval, opened his first retail shop, and brought in angel investors -- before he had even graduated.
First storefront Opened in Syracuse, New York -- the first step from a dorm-room delivery service to a real retail chain.

From one dorm room to a THE SCALE national brand

Milestone Detail

2018 Krispy Kreme -- yes, the doughnut company -- acquired a majority stake in Insomnia Cookies.
Locations today 265+ stores per Wikipedia, with more recent reporting citing 350+ locations across the US, Canada, and England.
Volume Over 20 million cookies sold every single quarter.
Where they are Major cities, suburbs, transportation hubs, stadiums -- far beyond the college campuses where it started.
Valuation Reported at roughly $350 million.
Ownership today Backed by Krispy Kreme alongside private equity firms Verlinvest and Mistral Equity Partners.

The Takeaway

A cookie. Not a new invention. Not a secret recipe nobody else could copy. Something sitting on a counter in front of millions of people, every single day, for decades. One person looked at it and simply asked why nobody had built a real brand around it -- and then did. The biggest opportunities are not always hidden. Sometimes they are sitting in plain sight, waiting for someone to actually build around them.
