KOLKATA AIRBNB AND REAL ESTATE GUIDE

Airbnb data: Airbtics Nov 2024-Oct 2025. Tourism: India Tourism Data Compendium 2025 (Union Tourism Ministry). Real estate: 99acres May 2026. Metro: realtypromoo, 99acres.

West Bengal was India's second highest state for foreign tourist arrivals in 2024, behind only Maharashtra. Kolkata achieves this despite minimal direct international flight connectivity, which suggests its true foreign footfall is understated. Airbnb in Kolkata is a high-demand, low-price market: strong occupancy, modest nightly rates. With the Orange Line metro targeted for December 2026 and property prices still the lowest of any major metro, the early-mover window is real. This guide covers the Airbnb market, tourism context, and real estate with verified data.

The undercounting point matters for an investor. Kolkata has almost no direct flights to Europe or the US, so many foreign tourists arrive via Mumbai or Delhi and get counted under those states. Despite that, West Bengal still ranks 2nd nationally. The drivers are durable and cultural: Durga Puja (now UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage), Tagore's legacy, colonial architecture, the Sundarbans, and Darjeeling. This is demand built on heritage, not hype.

The Kolkata thesis in one line: this is a volume market, not a rate market. At 45% median occupancy Kolkata sits in the top 30% of India for how often listings are booked, but ADR is in the lowest 15% nationally. You make money here by keeping the property booked, not by charging premium nightly rates. The operator who wins is the one who optimises for occupancy -- competitive pricing, fast responses, strong reviews -- rather than holding out for high nightly rates. Top quartile properties near heritage zones and business districts meaningfully outperform the median.

Five Things to Do Before You Sign Any Lease

-- Optimise for occupancy, not nightly rate. Kolkata is a volume market. Competitive pricing and fast guest response will beat holding out for premium rates. Your goal is to stay above the 45% median occupancy, not above the median ADR.

-- Position near Salt Lake Sector V or New Town for business and IT demand, or near Park Street and heritage zones for tourist demand. These two guest profiles want very different locations -- pick one and design for it.

  • -- Price hard for Durga Puja (Oct) and the Nov-Dec winter season. This is your peak window when the city floods with domestic and international visitors. Dynamic pricing in these weeks can carry a large share of annual revenue.

-- Factor in the Orange Line metro timing. If you can secure a property within 1 km of a New Town or Salt Lake Sector V station before December 2026, you capture both the connectivity uplift and the price re-rating that typically follows metro commissioning.

  • -- Confirm the landlord allows short-term rental and subletting in writing, and check the society or complex rules separately. This applies in Kolkata exactly as in any other Indian city.
