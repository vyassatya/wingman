Smash Guys

Business Research Report: Bengaluru's smash burger brand and the company behind it November 2026 | Based on public news reports, company statements and listing data. Estimates are marked.

1. The Short Version

  • A burger brand built on camera. Smash Guys started as a YouTube series called "The Burger Project" in 2024. The goal said out loud from day one: build a Rs 100 crore burger brand. The first outlet opened in Indiranagar, Bengaluru on 30 May 2025.

  • Small brand, big parent. Smash Guys is owned by Popo Global, which also runs The Pizza Bakery and Paris Panini. Popo Global has about 40 to 41 outlets, all in Bengaluru, and made about Rs 175 crore in revenue in FY25 with a profit.

  • Fresh money, first time. In September 2026, Popo Global raised Rs 532 crore (about $56 million) from Artal Asia. It was the company's first outside institutional money. One report puts the valuation at about Rs 1,250 crore.

  • Customers are showing up. Smash Guys grew from 1 to 4 dine-in outlets in about 17 months. Its Indiranagar outlet holds a 4.5 dining rating on Zomato from over 1,600 ratings, with queues on weekends.

  • The open question is scale. Smash Guys does not share its own sales. Service speed is the most common complaint. The new money is meant to take Popo Global's brands beyond Bengaluru, but no cities or outlet targets have been announced.

2. The Founders and the Origin Story

AB Gupta and Nikhil Gupta are brothers from Bengaluru. Both studied at Lancaster University in the United Kingdom. Nikhil worked at Procter & Gamble and then served as a Director at Cauvery Ford from 2014 to 2018. AB came from a more creative background and has run Travel Bazaar since 2011. Today AB is the public face of the group, with about 100,000 followers. He is known for his "Saturday Surprise Check" videos, where he walks into kitchens unannounced and checks standards.

Their first brand came from a trip to Delhi. The brothers went on a pizza tasting spree and saw that Bengaluru had no good gourmet pizza. They opened The Pizza Bakery in Indiranagar in 2017, selling wood-fired sourdough pizza. Paris Panini, a French-style sandwich brand, came next.

Smash Guys was brand number three, and AB wanted to build it differently. He filmed the whole process on YouTube, from recipe testing to interior design to hiring. He says he studied American burger history for about two years. He teamed up with Chef Adithya Kidambi, who trained at Le Cordon Bleu and ran a popular home burger kitchen during the pandemic. Before opening, the team ran burger tasting pop-ups. AB says those first 200 supporters grew to 800, then 3,000, so when the first outlet opened, customers were already waiting.

Smash Guys borrows a lot from its parent. Popo Global already had the kitchens, supply chain, hiring systems and delivery know-how from two earlier brands. That is why Smash Guys could go from one outlet to four in under a year and a half without outside money.

Earlier talks Moneycontrol reported talks with Invus Group for about Rs 500 crore at about Rs 1,100 crore valuation. Invus ended up as Artal's adviser
Advisers The Rainmaker Group advised Popo Global

What the numbers say. At Rs 1,250 crore, Popo Global is valued at about 7 times its FY25 revenue, or 5 times the Rs 250 crore run rate AB talks about. The company has not said how much of the Rs 532 crore is new money for growth and how much went to the founders as a share sale. If Rs 1,250 crore is the value after the deal, Artal owns about 43%. If it is the value before the deal, Artal owns about 30%.

One more point stands out. Artal Asia already backs Burger Singh, a low-priced burger chain with 185+ outlets. With Popo Global, the same investor now holds a premium burger brand too.

5. How a Smash Guys Outlet Makes Money

Smash Guys does not publish outlet numbers. The figures below are analyst estimates, built from the Rs 1,500 price for two and normal costs for premium burger restaurants in Indian metro cities. Treat them as a rough guide, not company data.

Line (per outlet, estimated) Share of sales What drives it
Food and packaging 30% to 32% Fresh meat patties, house-made buns, cheese
Staff 14% to 15% Open kitchen, table service, long hours
Rent and maintenance 12% to 13% High-street spots like Indiranagar and HSR
Delivery app commission 8% to 9% About 25% commission on the delivery share of sales
Power, gas and other costs About 8% Flat-top grills run hot all day
Marketing About 2% Low, because AB's content brings people in for free
Outlet profit before overheads About 20% What is left from each outlet

A busy outlet that serves about 180 dine-in guests and 100 delivery orders a day would bring in roughly Rs 60 lakh a month, or about Rs 7 crore a year. Newer outlets will start lower. If one outlet costs Rs 1 crore to Rs 1.5 crore to build, a good outlet can pay for itself in about 15 to 18 months.

6. Where Smash Guys Sits in the Burger Market

India's quick service restaurant market is worth about $27.8 billion in 2025 and is expected to reach about $43.5 billion by 2030, growing about 9.4% a year (Mordor Intelligence). Burgers are one of the most crowded parts of it. Most Indian burger chains compete on low prices and many outlets. Smash Guys competes on product and experience instead.

Brand Size Price position How it competes
McDonald's and Burger King Hundreds of outlets each Low to mid Speed, value, everywhere
Burger Singh 185+ outlets, 75+ cities Low Indian flavours at Indian prices
Biggies Burger 147+ outlets, 28 cities Low Franchise-led reach
Wat-a-Burger 65+ outlets Low to mid Mix of own and franchise outlets
Burgrill 50+ outlets Mid Between mass and premium
Beyondburg Small, Bengaluru Premium Smash burgers, chef-led
Smash Guys 4 dine-in outlets Premium American diner experience, founder- led content

The gap Smash Guys fills is the premium sit-down burger. Mass chains cannot easily move up here. Their kitchens are built for speed and low cost, not for fresh smashed patties, lamb and buff options, brisket slow-cooked for eight hours, and a full diner menu with bowls and mac and cheese. The main direct rival is Beyondburg, which is also small.

The parent company's numbers also need a careful read. News reports say Rs 175 crore in FY25 revenue with a profit. But the latest filings for one group company, Popo Ventures Private Limited, show almost no revenue for FY25. Most likely the restaurant sales sit in another company in the group, but the public record does not yet show this clearly.

8. Growth Plans in India

What the company has said: the Rs 532 crore will be used to open more outlets and take its brands beyond Bengaluru, all company-owned. It has not named target cities, outlet counts or a timeline. AB has said his next YouTube chapter is about taking Smash Guys to many outlets and sharing the problems that come with it.

Phase (analyst view) Likely moves What to watch
Now to mid-2027 Fill Bengaluru: more high-street and tech-park outlets, more delivery kitchens Can service keep up as outlets grow?
2027 to 2028 First cities outside Bengaluru, likely where The Pizza Bakery also goes Do sales per outlet hold outside home city?
2028 onward 15 to 25 outlets, closing in on the Rs 100 crore goal Does the founder's content still pull crowds at scale?
