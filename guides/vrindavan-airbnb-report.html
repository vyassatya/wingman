Tourist data: UP Tourism Department 2024. Airbnb data: Airbtics Jun 2024-May 2025. Real estate: 99acres May 2026. Infrastructure: YIAPL, UP Government.

Mathura received 9 crore tourists in 2024 per UP Tourism Department. Vrindavan has 236 active Airbnb listings (Airbtics, 2025). Visits by prominent public figures have driven a measurable uptick in millennial and Gen Z spiritual tourism to the region, creating demand for modern accommodation that the existing inventory -- largely pilgrimage-oriented dharamshalas and budget hotels -- does not serve. This guide covers the real estate market and the short-term rental opportunity with verified data.

Airbnb in Vrindavan is a festival-driven market. A host who prices dynamically for Holi, Janmashtami, and Kartik month and captures 3-4 peak festival weekends per year can significantly outperform the median Rs 3.76 lakh annual revenue. Conversely, a host who charges flat pricing year-round will underperform. Dynamic pricing is the single biggest lever available to a Vrindavan Airbnb operator.

Five Things to Do Before You Sign Any Lease

-- Confirm the landlord allows short-term rental and subletting in writing. Many Vrindavan landlords with religious or family properties have restrictions.

-- Position near Prem Mandir or Banke Bihari temple. These are the two locations modern visitors specifically search for. Proximity is the single biggest ADR driver in this market.

-- Use dynamic pricing for festival windows. Holi, Janmashtami, and Kartik month (Oct-Nov) are your three peak windows. Correct pricing in these windows alone changes your annual P&L; significantly.

-- Study the top 10 performing Vrindavan listings on Airbnb. Most will have a clean modern aesthetic with a spiritual design element -- not generic hotel-style.

-- Budget a 3-month runway beyond setup. First 30 days are slow before reviews accumulate. Reviews are critical in a pilgrimage market where trust is the primary booking driver.
