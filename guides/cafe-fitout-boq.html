This is not a generic industry estimate. The detailed cost-per-sqft breakup below was shared directly by Akash at Design Pulley, based on real fitout projects they have executed. Use this to sanity check any quote you receive before signing a fitout contract.

Detailed Cost-Per-Sqft Breakup (Premium Tier)

Source: Akash, Design Pulley -- itemised data shared directly. Total range: Rs 3,000-4,500/sqft.

Line Item Rs / Sqft On 2,000 sqft Notes
Finishing works (partition, paint, tiling, ceiling, glass) 900-1,300 Rs 18-26L Largest single line item
Movable furniture (chairs, tables, counter top, sofa) 700-1,000 Rs 14-20L Second largest. Do not under-budget here.
Facade, ACP, signage 250-400 Rs 5-8L Can vary significantly by spec
HVAC 350-450 Rs 7-9L
Electrical -- internal wiring, DB, switchboard 200-250 Rs 4-5L
CCTV, fire, PA (excl. sprinkler) 200-300 Rs 4-6L
Lightbox -- display, graphics, vinyl, props 100-250 Rs 2-5L Visual storytelling elements
Electrical -- all types of lights 100-200 Rs 2-4L
Civil works (cleaning, brickwork, debris removal, demolition, PCC, RCC) 100-300 Rs 2-6L Wide range -- site-dependent
Miscellaneous 100-200 Rs 2-4L
TOTAL 3,000-4,500 Rs 60-90L Sum of line items above

Finishing works and movable furniture together account for roughly 55 to 60% of the total budget in this premium tier. These are also the two line items that most directly affect what a guest sees and feels. Civil works has the widest range (Rs 100-300/sqft) because it depends entirely on the existing condition of the site -- a bare shell costs far less to prep than a space requiring demolition of prior fitouts.

How to Use This Template

-- Scale by area. For a space other than 2,000 sqft, multiply each per-sqft rate by your actual area to get item-wise and total budgets.

-- Compare any quote against this breakup. If finishing works or furniture is priced far outside Rs 900-1,300 and Rs 700-1,000 per sqft respectively, ask why before signing.

-- Site condition drives civil works cost more than design choices do. Get a site inspection before accepting any fixed quote on this line item.

-- Always add a 10-15% contingency on top of any quoted total. Site conditions, structural surprises, and design changes during execution are the norm, not the exception.
