Rishikesh has the highest average daily rate of any Tier 2 Airbnb market covered in this series at Rs 3,004 per night, driven by a strong international guest base at 33% of bookings. Uttarakhand crossed 6 crore tourist arrivals in 2025 for the first time. With 575 active Airbnb listings and a demand shift toward year-round weekend travel, the supply gap is meaningful. This guide covers the Airbnb market data, seasonality, setup budget, real estate prices, and what to check before signing a lease.

Rishikesh has the highest ADR of any Tier 2 city in this series at Rs 3,004 per night. The 33% international guest rate is second only to Varanasi. The 575 listing count is low relative to the destination scale -- Uttarakhand drew 6 crore visitors in 2025 -- but higher than Vrindavan and Indore, indicating a more developed market. The combination of high ADR and strong international demand creates pricing power that lower-ADR markets do not have.

Five Things to Do Before You Sign Any Lease

-- Confirm the landlord allows short-term rental and subletting in writing. Rishikesh has many property owners with conservative views on rentals, particularly near ashrams and temples.

-- Position near Tapovan or within 500 metres of Laxman Jhula. These are the two locations modern travellers specifically search for. Ganga view is your single biggest ADR lever -- it can add Rs 500 to Rs 1,500 per night over comparable non-view properties.

-- Price aggressively for the International Yoga Festival in March, October-November post-monsoon, and New Year. These three windows can represent 40% of your annual revenue if priced correctly.

-- Plan the monsoon window honestly. July to mid-September is your dead period. Either budget for 2 to 2.5 months of zero or near-zero occupancy or use this window for maintenance, deep cleaning, and getting more 5-star reviews from non-monsoon guests.

-- International guest readiness matters here more than any other city in this series. 33% of your guests will be foreign. English communication, clear check-in instructions, Western bathroom amenities, and fast WiFi are non-negotiable.
