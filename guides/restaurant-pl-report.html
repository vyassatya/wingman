These are companies with decades of history, hundreds of outlets, PE backing, and global franchise agreements. Their margins tell the only story that matters.

McDonald's India made 0.5% net margin in FY25. Barbeque Nation made a net loss. SOCIAL -- the cafe everyone wants to copy -- took 24 years and a Rs 100 crore PE injection to make its first profit. If these companies with hundreds of outlets, global brands, and PE backing cannot consistently make money, the deck is structurally stacked against a first-time operator at a prime location.

The Bottom Line

-- Build from genuine passion for food. This is not a business you can run from a spreadsheet.

-- Innovate on the menu. The customer has too many options to return for something ordinary.

-- Build a differentiated product. Own a format, a cuisine, or an experience nobody else has.

-- Plan to stay long. Every brand in this report that survived did so over years and decades, not months.

-- This is not a fad business. Do not treat it like one.
