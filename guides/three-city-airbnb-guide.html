Five Things to Do Before You Sign Any Lease

  • -- Visit at least 3 properties in person or via video call. Do not commit to photos alone.

  • -- Check the landlord specifically allows short-term rental or subletting. Not all do. Get it in writing.

  • -- Walk the neighbourhood. Car access, parking, proximity to transport. Guests will ask all of this.

  • -- Research the 10 best performing Airbnb listings in that area. Study their photos, pricing, and reviews.

  • -- Build a 3-month runway buffer on top of your setup cost. Revenue takes time to stabilise.
