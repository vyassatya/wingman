BUSINESSES YOU CAN BUILD FOR THE KIDS

1. TARANIS -- CHINA -- KIDS FUNCTIONAL FOOTWEAR

Metric Detail
Founded 2011, Hangzhou. Brand of Hangzhou Taitong Trading Co., Ltd.
Category Functional shoes for ages 0-16, engineered around 6 growth stages of the foot ("1/3FLEX" technology).
Scale (2024) Nearly 1,000 stores across 360 Chinese cities; mid-to-high-end mall channel positioning.
2021 sales RMB 500 million (approx. Rs 715 crore at Rs 14.3/CNY) reported for FY2021.
International expansion First overseas flagship opened in California, USA, in 2024. Officially licensed Minions & Monsters kids footwear collection with Universal launched July 2026.

2. HIYA -- USA -- KIDS NUTRACEUTICALS / VITAMINS

Metric Detail
Founded 2019 (launched March 2020) by Darren Litt and Adam Gillman. Self-funded -- built without traditional venture capital.
Category Chewable multivitamins for kids -- no added sugar, artificial dyes or gummies. Subscription (DTC) model.
Revenue (LTM to Sep 2024) Net sales USD 103 million (approx. Rs 989 crore); net income USD 19M (approx. Rs 182 crore); adjusted EBITDA USD 22M (approx. Rs 211 crore).
Customers 200,000+ as of September 2024.
Acquisition USANA Health Sciences (NYSE: USNA) acquired a 78.8% controlling stake for USD 205 million cash (approx. Rs 1,968 crore), closed Dec 23, 2024. Reported overall deal/company valuation USD 260 million (approx. Rs 2,496 crore).

3. ONCE UPON A FARM -- USA -- CLEAN-LABEL KIDS FOOD

Metric Detail
Founded 2015 by Cassandra Curtis and Ari Raz. Jennifer Garner and John Foraker (ex-CEO, Annie's) joined as co-founders in 2017.
Category Organic, cold-pressed baby food and kids' snacks -- pouches, oat bars, dairy-style products. No added sugar or preservatives.
Total funding USD 108 million raised over 5 rounds (approx. Rs 1,037 crore), incl. a USD 52M Series D led by CAVU Consumer Partners.
Revenue (FY ended Sep 2025) USD 225 million (approx. Rs 2,160 crore) annual revenue, up 40%+ YoY; >60% revenue CAGR since 2018.
Distribution ~25,000 stores nationwide incl. Target, Whole Foods, Kroger, Walmart, Publix, Wegmans.

Listed on NYSE as "OFRM," Feb 2026, at USD 18/share. IPO raised USD 198M; company valued at USD 724 million (approx. Rs 6,950 crore) at listing.

4. TIN CAN -- USA -- SCREEN-FREE KIDS COMMUNICATION

Metric Detail
Founded 2022-23, Seattle, by Chet Kittleson, Max Blumen and Graeme Davies (Kittleson previously founded real-estate startup Far Homes).
Category Wi-Fi-enabled, screen-free "landline-style" phone for kids aged 4-13. No apps, browsing or texting -- calls only, to a parent-approved contact list via companion app.
Price point ~USD 100 per device (approx. Rs 9,600) plus a free "Can2Can" calling plan between devices.
Total funding USD 21.5 million raised across 4 rounds -- USD 3.5M pre-seed (Sep 2025) and a USD 12M seed round led by Greylock Partners (Dec 2025). Investors include Greylock, a16z-adjacent Antler, Mother Ventures.
Traction Reached users in all 50 US states and Canada; sold out multiple production batches, incl. at Christmas 2025.
Revenue / unit sales Not disclosed. No revenue, unit-sales or profitability figures were found in any credible source at the time of this report.
