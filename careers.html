We are building a team across health, fitness, nutrition and digital content. We are looking for ambitious individuals who are passionate about their craft, take ownership of their work and enjoy working in a fast-paced entrepreneurial environment.

Video Editor

Key responsibilities:

  • Edit engaging Instagram reels, YouTube videos and branded content.
  • Create compelling storytelling through editing, transitions, sound design and motion graphics.
  • Understand social media trends, audience retention and short-form content.
  • Maintain consistent visual quality across all content.

Preferred qualifications: Experience with Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve or similar editing tools. A strong portfolio is more important than formal qualifications.

Videographer

Key responsibilities:

  • Shoot high-quality content for social media, brand campaigns and product launches.
  • Manage camera equipment, lighting, framing and audio.
  • Shoot founder-led content, interviews, fitness and lifestyle videos.
  • Collaborate with the creative team on concepts and execution.

Preferred qualifications: Experience shooting digital-first content, with a strong understanding of lighting, composition and visual storytelling.

Personal Fitness Trainer

Key responsibilities:

  • Design personalised fitness and transformation programmes for clients.
  • Conduct virtual or in-person training sessions.
  • Monitor client progress, performance and adherence.
  • Collaborate with nutritionists and healthcare professionals to deliver outcome-driven transformation programmes.

Preferred qualifications: Recognised fitness certification, strong knowledge of strength training and experience working with individual clients.

Dietitian

Key responsibilities:

  • Develop personalised nutrition plans based on client goals, lifestyle and relevant health assessments.
  • Support clients through weight management, muscle building and overall health improvement programmes.
  • Monitor progress and periodically revise nutrition plans.
  • Collaborate with fitness trainers and medical professionals.

Preferred qualifications: Relevant degree or recognised qualification in nutrition or dietetics, with experience in personalised nutrition and lifestyle management.
