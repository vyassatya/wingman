Pizza 4P's

Company Research Report: The India Business

For founders and operators who want to learn how a premium restaurant brand enters India | November 2026

1. Executive Summary

Pizza 4P's is a pizza chain started in Vietnam in 2011 by a Japanese couple. It now runs about 40 restaurants in six countries. India is its smallest market by store count, but it is the market the founder chose to live in. Here is the story in five points.

  • Two stores in three years, and still hard to get a table. Pizza 4P's opened in Indiranagar, Bengaluru in December 2023 and added a second store at Bagmane Solarium City in April 2026. Bookings open only one week ahead, and the Indiranagar store has more than 13,000 Google ratings at an average of 4.8.

  • The cheese is the strategy. The team says it was the first to make cheese from buffalo milk in India. At Indiranagar, guests can watch fresh cheese being made in a cheese lab. This is hard to copy and it is the main reason people talk about the brand.

  • The menu was rebuilt for India. Vegetarian dishes went from under 30% of the menu to 50%. New pizzas like Paneer Makhani and Tokyo Chicken Curry were added after five months of weekend tastings.

  • The founder moved to Bengaluru. Chief Executive Officer Yosuke Masuko lists Bengaluru as his home. The India company's share capital went from Rs 13 crore to Rs 30 crore. This is a long-term bet, not a test store.

  • Small revenue today, strong store economics. The India company reported under Rs 10 crore in revenue for FY24, which covered only about four months of trading. Our estimate puts each Indian store at Rs 9 to 13 crore a year, close to the group average of about USD 2 million per store.

2. The Global Business in Brief

2.1 Founder and origin story

Yosuke Masuko worked at a trading company and then at CyberAgent, a Japanese internet company, where he ran its venture investing arm in Vietnam from 2008. In 2005 he had built a wood-fired oven in his backyard in Tokyo and hosted weekly pizza parties for friends. He named the idea "Pizza for Peace", which became 4P's. In 2011 he and his wife Sanae Takasugi quit their jobs and opened the first store in a small alley on Le Thanh Ton Street in Ho Chi Minh City. They lived in a small room above the restaurant with their young daughter.

Fresh mozzarella was hard to find in Vietnam, so in 2012 Masuko spent about USD 100,000 on a small cheese workshop. The team taught itself from YouTube videos and rode motorbikes to small dairy farms to find good milk. That workshop is now a cheese factory in Da Lat that makes burrata, camembert, blue cheese and more.

3. How Pizza 4P's Entered India

Masuko links India to a stay in Rishikesh, where he learned the idea of "Universal Love". That idea later became the company's core belief of "Oneness". The team says it chose India because it is very diverse but still holds together.

Date Milestone

4. The India Playbook: What They Changed

Most foreign pizza chains come to India with a fixed menu and add a paneer topping. Pizza 4P's did the opposite. It rebuilt the supply chain and the menu around what India already has.

Area What they did Why it matters
Cheese Makes six types of cheese every day in-store from fresh local milk. Uses buffalo milk, which has 7 to 8% fat, about double cow's milk. The team drove up to 8 hours from Bengaluru to find farms Buffalo milk is cheap and easy to get in India. It makes richer mozzarella and burrata, and gives a story no rival has
Menu mix Vegetarian share raised to 50%, from under 30% in other markets Matches how Indian groups order. One vegetarian guest no longer limits the table
New dishes Paneer Makhani pizza with 10 spices and green yogurt chutney. Tokyo Chicken Curry pizza mixing Japanese curry, Thai paste and garam masala Familiar flavours lower the risk of trying a new brand
Local suppliers Araku Coffee, Sula Vineyards wine, Local Ferment Co., Mountain Bee Kombucha, Green Apron mushrooms, LeafyHydroponics greens Lower import costs and duties, and a farm-to-table story that fits the premium price
Design Plates from Mattipilla Ceramics in Hyderabad, textiles from Minanco Home and baskets from Nila House in Jaipur The store feels Indian, not imported
Price About Rs 2,000 for two people Premium casual dining. Above Domino's and Pizza Hut, close to Bengaluru's craft pizzerias

5. How the India Business Is Doing

5.1 Demand signals
Signal What the evidence shows
Customer ratings Indiranagar has more than 13,000 Google ratings with an average of 4.8 stars, which is rare for a restaurant open under three years
Booking pressure Reservations open at 10 AM daily for the same day next week. The booking page warns against resold tables. In September 2026, a Bengaluru diner posted that he flew to Tokyo to eat at Pizza 4P's because he could not get a table at home, and the founder replied to apologise
Media and lists Time Out named Bengaluru the 13th best food city in the world in 2026, and coverage of that list named the Pizza 4P's garlic shrimp and burrata pizzas as must- try dishes. It is not yet on the 50 Top Pizza Asia-Pacific list, where India's three entries are Da Susy, Leo's Pizzeria and Cucina Marinara
Complaints The most common complaints are small portions for the price and a hard-to-reach reservation team. Both are signs of a store running over capacity
5.2 Financials

4Ps India Private Limited files accounts with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs. Its FY24 revenue, for the year to March 2024, was under Rs 10 crore. That year included only about four months of trading from one store. FY25 and FY26 figures are not yet public in free databases, so we built an estimate for one mature store.

Guests per day (about 120 seats) 250 300 350

Seat count and daily guests are our assumptions, based on a three-floor building with fully booked dinners. Spend per guest comes from the published Rs 2,000 for two. The base case is about USD 1.3 million, a little below the group average of USD 2 million per store. The gap is mostly lower prices in India. With two stores, the India business is likely running at Rs 18 to 25 crore a year in 2026.

6. Growth Plans in India

Pizza 4P's has not announced a store target for India. What it has done says more than a press release. The founder lives in Bengaluru, capital has more than doubled, and the second store went into an office campus, not a high street. That points to a plan of growing slowly, store by store, owned by the company and not franchised.

Phase What we expect What has to be true
Now to 2027 Two to four stores in Bengaluru. Office campuses and premium high streets like Whitefield, Koramangala and Hebbal The cheese room can supply more than one store, and the second store fixes its early delays
2027 to 2029 A second city, most likely Mumbai or Delhi NCR A steady buffalo milk supply near the new city. North India has more buffalo farms than the south, which helps
Later Packaged cheese and frozen pizza for premium grocery stores, as the brand already does in Vietnam Enough store-level brand recognition to sell on shelves
6.1 Competition

India's mass pizza market is slowing. Pizza Hut's restaurant revenue fell 5% and same-store sales fell 8% in Q1 FY26 for Sapphire Foods India. New global entrants are also aiming low and wide: Papa Johns came back to Bengaluru in October 2025 with four stores and a target of 650 stores by 2034, Little Caesars opened in Gurugram in June 2025, and &pizza plans to open in Mumbai in 2027. Pizza 4P's does not fight these brands. Its real rivals are local craft pizzerias such as Leo's Pizzeria, Da Susy and Bengaluru's own Neapolitan spots, and the wider premium dining spend in the city.

7. Key Risks

Risk Why it matters What Pizza 4P's can do
Milk quality and supply The whole brand rests on fresh cheese. Buffalo milk varies by season and farm Sign long-term deals with a few farms and build a central cheese kitchen before city two
Hype fades Long waits create buzz now but can turn into frustration Add seats and stores before repeat guests give up
Price and portion pushback Rs 2,000 for two is high for pizza in India Keep a few lower-priced items and push half-and-half pizzas
Slow openings The second store opened six months late. Each delay costs rent and staff time Use a standard store format for future sites

8. Closing Note

Pizza 4P's shows a different way to enter India. It did not chase store counts or delivery apps. It sent the founder, rebuilt the cheese around buffalo milk, made half the menu vegetarian, and let one store prove the demand before opening a second.

The numbers are still small, but the signals are strong: a full book, high ratings and a parent company putting in more money. If the cheese supply can scale, India can become one of the group's largest markets by the end of the decade.
